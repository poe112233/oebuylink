FAQ

FAQ

What is a Buylink conversion?
It turns a Weidian product link into a purchase link that works directly on each buying platform. Formats differ per platform; this tool generates them all automatically.
Which marketplace links are supported?
Weidian product links in the form weidian.com/item.html?itemID=<item id>, one per line, batch friendly.
Which buying platforms are supported?
OOPBUY, KAKOBUY, UIDBUY, HIPOBUY, LITBUY and BOONBUY — each with its own landing page, reachable from the top navigation or the sitemap.
Does the tool upload my links?
No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser; nothing is sent to any server, and it works offline.
How do I pick the right platform?
Generate links for all 6 platforms at once and compare fees, promotions and workflow. Each landing page has a comparison table and a detailed guide.
Where is the sitemap?
All pages (including both language versions of every platform page) are listed at oebuylink.com/sitemap.html, with the XML version at oebuylink.com/sitemap.xml.
