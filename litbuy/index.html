FAQ

LITBUY FAQ

What jewelry is available on LITBUY?
Necklaces, bracelets and earrings, plus Van Cleef & Arpels and Bvlgari collections — over 66 jewelry styles are listed on the main store.
How do I buy a necklace on LITBUY?
Pick a style on ores.cc → open its product page → click the LITBUY purchase entry → confirm length and material, then check out. Inspection and photos are available on arrival.
What should I check during jewelry inspection?
Look for oxidation, plating, settings and clasp craftsmanship. Review the inspection photos carefully before deciding to consolidate the shipment.
Can jewelry ship with other items?
Yes. Jewelry is small and light, ideal for consolidated shipping with apparel and bags, billed by actual weight; see LITBUY's official site for details.
