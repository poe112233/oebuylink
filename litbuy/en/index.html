FAQ

LITBUY FAQ

What is the linkSearch parameter in a LITBUY link?
A LITBUY purchase link looks like litbuy.com/product/weidian/<itemID>?linkSearch=true. The linkSearch parameter lets the platform look up the item directly from the source link, which is especially useful in shops with large catalogs.
How do I buy a Weidian product with LITBUY?
Four steps: copy the Weidian link → generate the LITBUY purchase link with the tool on this page → open the link, sign in and order → choose inspection and consolidation on arrival. Everything runs in Chinese with trackable shipping.
How do I generate a LITBUY purchase link?
Paste the Weidian link into the converter at the top of this page and click "Generate links". It runs entirely in your browser and uploads nothing; you can also visit https://litbuy.com directly.
How do I choose between LITBUY and other platforms?
Compare the same item across all 6 platforms on price and speed. The comparison table below summarizes each platform's purchase method — click a platform name for its full landing page.
What about shipping fees and delivery time?
Fees, timing and warehouse policies vary by platform and change with promotions — always check LITBUY's official site. This tool only generates purchase links and is not part of the transaction.
