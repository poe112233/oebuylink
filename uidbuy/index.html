FAQ

UIDBUY FAQ

Which watch brands are available on UIDBUY?
Rolex, Audemars Piguet, Patek Philippe, Richard Mille, Cartier, Breguet, Piaget and Vacheron Constantin are all listed, with over 256+ watches on the main store.
How do I buy a watch on UIDBUY?
Pick a reference on ores.cc → open its product page → click the UIDBUY purchase entry → confirm the model and accessories, then check out. Inspection and photos are available on arrival.
Can watches be inspected at the warehouse?
Yes. Watches are high-value items, so inspection and photo services on arrival are strongly recommended before consolidation, subject to UIDBUY's official policies.
Do all references come with the same accessories?
No. Cards, boxes and spare straps vary by reference. Check the product photos and description on the ores.cc product page before ordering.
Is this page affiliated with UIDBUY?
No. This is an Oriental Essence product guide page with no affiliation to UIDBUY; all products and purchase entries come from the main store ores.cc.
