FAQ

BOONBUY FAQ

What are the most popular products on BOONBUY?
Based on sales data from the main store, sneakers, streetwear and watches are the three hottest directions; this best-seller list covers Nike, Air Jordan, YEEZY, Gucci, Rolex and more.
How do I buy a best-seller on BOONBUY?
Pick a style on ores.cc → open its product page → search the same item by name or model on the official BOONBUY site → confirm and order. Inspection and consolidation are available on arrival.
Is BOONBUY's link format the same as other platforms?
Yes. BOONBUY uses boonbuy.com/product/weidian/<itemID> style Weidian direct links, the same format as OOPBUY and HIPOBUY.
Do best-sellers run out of sizes?
Popular sizes and colorways move fast. Bookmark the product page on ores.cc to catch restocks, or pick alternatives from this list.
Is this page affiliated with BOONBUY?
No. This is an Oriental Essence product guide page with no affiliation to BOONBUY; all products and purchase entries come from the main store ores.cc.
